Travel & Getting There

Flight Directions

Option A: Fly to Sanya (SYX)

Book a flight to Sanya Phoenix International Airport (SYX). It is the most convenient option, located about a 40-minute taxi/Didi ride (approx. 35km) from The St. Regis Resort Yalong Bay.

Option B: Fly to Haikou (HAK)

For more flight schedules, fly to Haikou Meilan International Airport (HAK). Walk directly to the Haikou Meilan Airport High-Speed Train Station and take a high-speed train to Sanya Station (takes 1.5 to 2 hours). From Sanya Station, take a 25-minute taxi to Yalong Bay.

Hainan 59-Country Visa-Free Entry

Important for International Guests

Hainan Province offers a **59-country visa-free policy** for tourists. Citizens from eligible countries (including USA, Canada, UK, Australia, Singapore, EU countries, etc.) can travel to Hainan **visa-free for up to 30 days** if they enter directly from international locations.

Note: Registration through a Hainan-licensed travel agency is required before arrival. Please consult your airline or travel agent to complete this registration at least 48 hours before departure.

Traveling to Mainland China & Extended Stays

If you wish to extend your trip and visit other cities in Mainland China (such as Shanghai, Beijing, or Guangzhou) before or after our wedding, you have two primary options:

Option A: 144-Hour Visa-Free Transit (Layovers)

Citizens of 54 countries (including USA, Canada, UK, Australia, Singapore) can stay in designated mainland regions for up to 144 hours (6 days) without a visa. You must arrive from Country/Region A, transit through China, and depart to a different Country/Region B.

Visa-Free Loophole: Because Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan count as separate regions, you can structure a route like USA → Shanghai (144h transit) → Hong Kong → Sanya (30-day Hainan visa-free) → USA to visit both regions completely visa-free!

Option B: Standard Chinese Tourist Visa (L Visa)

If you plan to fly domestically within China (e.g., flying from Beijing → Shanghai → Sanya) or stay longer than 6 days in mainland China, you must apply for a standard L Tourist Visa at a Chinese consulate before your trip.

Tip: US and Canadian citizens are typically eligible for 10-year multiple-entry visas, allowing unlimited domestic travel within China for up to 60 days per entry.

Local Transit in Sanya

  • Didi (Ride-hailing): The Chinese equivalent of Uber. Accessible directly via the WeChat or Alipay apps (which support foreign credit cards). Highly recommended for easy navigation!
  • Taxis: Readily available at Sanya Airport and Sanya Train Station. Simply show the resort name in Chinese to the driver: 三亚亚龙湾瑞吉度假酒店.
